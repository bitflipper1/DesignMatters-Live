About Me

I'm Matt McGlothlin, a product and UX designer based in Charlotte, NC.

I've worked with companies ranging from Fortune 100 giants like Bank of America, Honeywell, and Wells Fargo to startups like ecomdash. Along the way I've served as a Senior UX Designer and Lead UX Designer, shipping everything from enterprise platforms to mobile apps.

I've also started several companies of my own: RepRevive (an online reputation management tool), AdSpark (promotional products and store integration), and Bitflip (website design, development, and hosting).

My current passion is design systems and leveraging automation to build more consistent interfaces across enterprises.
