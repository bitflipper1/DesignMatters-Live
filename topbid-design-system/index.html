TopBid Design System

A design system that eliminated communication friction between the product team and offshore developers.

The Challenge

The TopBid product team experienced delays in development with offshore developers due to communication and time differences. Requirements were interpreted inconsistently, and rework was common.

The Solution

A design system was discovered as a great way to solve communication issues with developers in other time zones. Components, patterns, and specifications became the shared source of truth for the whole team.

The Outcome

The development process became much more efficient, and the product team launched the new TopBid interface using Agile to work with developers and business analysts.
