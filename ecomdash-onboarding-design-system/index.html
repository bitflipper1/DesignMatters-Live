Ecomdash – Onboarding & Design System

Redesigning and modernizing the e-commerce management platform through research, data-driven design, and design-system standardization.

The Product

Ecomdash helps small to mid-size online retailers automate listings, inventory, and order fulfillment across channels like Amazon, eBay, and Shopify.

The Mission

From 2019–2021, the mission was to redesign and modernize the platform — improving usability and conversion rates through research, data-driven design, and design-system standardization.

The Outcome

A standardized component library, a streamlined onboarding experience, and measurable gains in usability and conversion across the platform.
