RepRevive.com · iOS · Startup · Founder

Reputation Management Mobile App

An iOS app for Charlotte-based startup RepRevive.com — reputation management software for business.

The Product

RepRevive.com is a Charlotte-based startup providing a reputation management software solution for business. The app sends a frictionless survey to customers and, based on their response, encourages them to leave feedback, follow on social media, or leave a review.

The Design

The iOS experience was designed around a single principle: remove friction. Surveys complete in seconds, and happy customers are routed straight to the review platforms that matter most to the business.

The Outcome

A simple, focused mobile product that turns everyday customer interactions into reviews, followers, and actionable feedback.
