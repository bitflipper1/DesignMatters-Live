EDA Responsive Redesign

Modernizing a legacy web experience into a fully responsive, device-agnostic design.

The Challenge

A legacy fixed-width experience was failing users on modern devices. The redesign set out to deliver the full experience responsively — from desktop to tablet to phone.

The Approach

A responsive grid, flexible components, and a mobile-first content hierarchy brought the experience up to modern standards while preserving the workflows users depended on.
